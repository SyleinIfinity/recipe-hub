# FE-04-VR — Prompts và provenance

Công cụ: built-in `image_gen.imagegen` qua skill imagegen, `transparent_background: true`, không input image/edit target. Ba artwork gốc riêng, không dùng master screenshot như dữ liệu đầu vào generation. Không dùng CLI/API key hoặc stock. Đã xem output bằng view_image trước khi export.

Export dùng Pillow chỉ đổi kích thước toàn ảnh và mã hóa WebP quality86/method6, giữ alpha, không crop/composite/đổi nội dung. PNG nguồn giữ nguyên tại đường dẫn trong generated-art-manifest; sáu WebP dùng trong project nằm trong frontend/public/assets/kitchen. Kích thước/bytes/hash của cả source và export được ghi trong manifest.

## Window — exact prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original transparent decorative layer for a cozy illustrated 2D kitchen web application, NOT a webpage or UI. Draw ONLY a tall wooden kitchen window with softly tied ivory linen curtains, warm honey oak recessed frame with perspective thickness, four panes, soft sunlit layered green garden outside, three terracotta pots with naturally varied leafy herbs on a thick wooden sill. Composition: isolated complete object, near frontal, slight handcrafted perspective, portrait 3:4 object centered with very little transparent padding, no surrounding room/wall/floor. Warm soft painterly gouache storybook interior, natural brush texture, organic imperfect edges, ochre highlights, moss and olive foliage, warm cream curtains, soft ambient occlusion. Gentle afternoon sunlight from upper left. Crisp silhouette suitable at 280 CSS pixels wide. Genuine transparent background, preserve complete curtains and sill without clipping. No text, letters, logo, controls, people, food photography, 3D, flat vector, watermark. This is a new original artwork, not a copy of any existing screenshot.

## Stove — exact prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original transparent composite decorative layer for a warm illustrated 2D kitchen website, NOT a UI mockup. Draw an isolated narrow cooking nook: brushed warm gray stove extractor hood at top; one honey oak shelf with a few unlabeled glass spice jars and a trailing olive green vine; simple black hanging utensil bar with two pans, wooden spoon and spatula; cream enamel stovetop and front of an oven below; a prominent burnt orange cast iron pot with lid and dark handles on the stovetop. Composition: near frontal, slight handcrafted perspective, portrait 3:4, complete silhouette centered with minimal transparent padding, no wall/backdrop/floor, no extra counter stretching sideways. Cozy soft painterly gouache storybook illustration, organic uneven outlines, subtle brush texture, detailed materials, soft amber highlights and warm deep shadows, layered depth, restrained wood/cream/terracotta/olive palette. Soft sunlight from upper left. Orange pot must be clearly visible in the lower third and dimensional, not neon. Genuine transparent background. No text, logos, labels, buttons, cards, people, photography, 3D, flat vector, watermark. New original production artwork.

## Countertop — exact prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original panoramic transparent foreground counter layer for a cozy illustrated 2D kitchen website. Canvas wide landscape, approximately 4:1 aspect ratio. Draw a long honey oak kitchen island worktop seen from very slightly above and almost straight in front. It spans the full width of the canvas edge to edge with a thick rounded front edge and a shallow warm dark wooden apron below. Not a table with legs. Show a generous golden top surface in shallow perspective, subtle organic wood grain and worn edges, soft warm highlights on the rear/top and darker ambient occlusion under the front edge. At the far left only: one small wooden cutting board, one ripe tomato and half tomato, a loosely draped red and cream checked linen tea towel hanging over the front edge, a few herb leaves. Keep center/right worktop clear. Style: soft painterly gouache storybook interior, handcrafted brush textures and natural imperfect shapes, warm wood/cream/amber palette, light from upper left, beautiful dimensional illustration, NOT flat vector, NOT photorealistic or 3D. Entire complete object occupies most of the wide canvas, minimal transparent padding all around. Genuine transparent background above/below; no wall, room, people, words, UI, text, logo, watermark, controls, recipe photos, cupboards, extra furniture or legs. This is a new isolated original decorative layer, not a screenshot or interface.
